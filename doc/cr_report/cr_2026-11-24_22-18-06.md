```rust

collect_references(&text, &mut references);
let gb_text = crate::doc::decode_with_hint(&bytes, Some(gb_hint))
    .text
    .to_ascii_lowercase();

    collect_references(&gb_text, &mut references);
```

- **状态**：已修复（新增 `gb18030_note_keeps_chinese_asset` 回归测试）
